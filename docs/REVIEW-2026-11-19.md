- **Widths 360 / 390 / 768 / 1440 px:** no horizontal scrolling on the homepage, filtered shop, zero-result filter, iPhone 16, iPhone 15, iPad Air M3, basket, checkout or Versand & Zahlung.
- **Full-device framing:** passes for all 16 listings, on both the product page and the card, at 390 and 1440 px.
- **Variable iPad Air M3:** 11 Zoll · 128 GB · Blau · Wi-Fi → 599,00 €, "1 vorrätig", Art.-Nr. GAD-IPADAIR-M3-11-128-BLU-WIFI. The sold-out combination cannot be added to the basket.

- **Trade-in:** blocked without a model. "Tablet · iPad Air · 128 GB · Gut, leichte Gebrauchsspuren" appears identically in the preview and the copied text.
- **Keyboard:** all options are focusable buttons (arrow keys move within a group), and the hidden native selects are taken out of the tab order. A "Zum Inhalt springen" (skip to content) link is in place.
- **Motion fallback:** a static frame when "reduce motion" is set. This was implemented, but not checked on a device with the setting switched on.
- **Long German labels:** wrap without overflow (checked at 360 px).

## Operational inputs we need from you (one list)
1. **Company (after the notary):** legal name and legal form, address, representative(s), register court and number, VAT ID, person responsible for content, contact e-mail, telephone, opening hours (if there is a shop).
2. **WhatsApp business number** for product questions, support and trade-in.
3. **Shipping:** zones / countries, rate per zone and method, free-shipping threshold (if any), delivery time, carrier, insured shipping.
4. **Payment methods** and provider accounts. Only "Vorkasse" is active, as a test, with no bank details.
5. **Returns:** return address, who pays return costs, process and processing time.
6. **Warranty:** provider, duration, coverage, how to claim (kept separate from statutory rights).
7. **VAT:** are refurbished units sold under the margin scheme (§ 25a UStG)?
8. **Prices, SKUs and stock** for every listing (all current values are placeholders).
9. **Buyback:** which devices and tablet brands you accept.
